Before you sign

A wallet prompt from confetti.trade only ever names an address on this page. Approvals are for the exact amount you typed, never unlimited, and the site never asks you to sign a message or a permit. If a prompt names any other address, stop and tell us on @confetti_trade.

New sites can get a notice from a wallet's scanner until the scanner knows them. Each contract below has its full source published and matched byte for byte to what is on chain.
