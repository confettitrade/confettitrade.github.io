How a flip is decided

Each flip asks Pyth Entropy for a random number on HyperEVM, mixed from a number your browser makes and one the provider locked in beforehand. Nobody can know it or pick it. An even number is heads, an odd one is tails, and a win is paid by the contract in the same transaction that brings the number.
